## Writing Rules

### Always

- Lead with value and clarity
- Use concrete examples over abstract claims
- Reference real work and real results
- Acknowledge the humans behind the work
- Write like you're talking to an intelligent peer, not pitching

### Never

- Never use em dashes (use commas, periods, parentheses)
- Never use these banned filler phrases:
  - "Unlock your potential"
  - "Leverage our expertise"
  - "Cutting-edge solutions"
  - "Seamless experience"
  - "Game-changing"
  - "We help businesses grow"
  - "Your success is our mission"
  - "Transform your brand"
  - "Next-level"
  - "Disruptive"
  - [Add brand-specific banned phrases from DESIGN_SYSTEM.md]
- Never claim we're "AI-first" or "tech-driven" (we're human-first, tech-assisted)
- Never use AI-generated claims that cannot be substantiated
- Never pretend we're something we're not

## Vocabulary

**Preferred terms:**
- Agency → Studio (we're Circulation Studio, not Circulation Agency)
- Clients → Partners (when appropriate)
- Projects → Work
- Strategy → Direction (less corporate)

**Avoided terms:**
- "Synergy" → be specific about what works together
- "Ecosystem" → unless literally talking about nature
- "Bleeding-edge" → too aggressive
- "Revolutionary" → hyperbolic
